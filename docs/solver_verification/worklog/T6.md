## Part A — ledger completeness

Terms added to `thermal_source` (all in `_goodman_ignition_sources_and_mass` / the pyrogen block of `_run_time_loop`):
| Term | sign | channel (history) |
|---|---|---|
| propellant sidewall `prop_source·T_flame·Cp_prop` | + | `normal_/erosive_sidewall_thermal_power` |
| end-face `endface_msource·T_flame·Cp_prop` | + | `endface_thermal_power` |
| adjacent radiation sink `radiation_sink_power/dx` | − | `radiation_sink_power` |
| **convective wall sink `h_conv·ΔT·C_burn`** | − | **new `convective_wall_sink_power`** (only term lacking one) |
| pyrogen enthalpy (plenum weights / uncontained cells) | + | `pyrogen_enthalpy_power` |
| pyrogen surface heat sink (cell 0 / cartridge cells) | − | `gas_surface_heat_sink_power` |

`source_ledger_mismatch_W = normal+erosive+endface+pyrogen_enthalpy − radiation_sink − gas_surface_heat_sink − convective_wall_sink − thermal_source_power`.

**Nozzle ledger (by-design difference).** PISO's `nozzle_scalar_flux_power` is evaluated explicitly from the pre-clamp `P_new` and the START-of-step `T`; the STEP-6 reconstruction (what `massflow`/`total_mass_nozzle` use) uses the final (clamped) `P` and END-of-step `T`. They differ by the one-step change of the cell N−1 state. Outside the fill it is ≤ 14–30 W against ~1e7 W (plateau/spike max: Chunc 29 / 14 W; BALLS 28 / 17 W); in the fill it reaches 2.2–3.4 kW. The same effect gives a nozzle MASS mismatch `massflow_piso − massflow`: max 4.2e-3 kg/s (Chunc), 7.2e-3 kg/s (BALLS) — i.e. the mass the gas ledger removes through the nozzle is not exactly the mass `total_mass_nozzle` accumulates (explicit-vs-implicit nozzle state, not a leak). The Codex 2.05e-8 W number is the telescoping interior-flux check (`convective + nozzle` ≈ 0, which our `budget` shows to round-off), a different comparison.

**FINDING (not fixed, outside T6's scope).** With `radiation_emissivity > 0` the ledger exposes a real defect: in the Goodman loop the sink on the LEFT neighbour (`radiation_sink_power[i-1] += …`) is accumulated after cell i−1's `thermal_source[i-1] -= radiation_sink_power[i-1]/dx` already ran, so it is recorded but never debited. Chunc t_max=0.05, emissivity 0.05: max source-ledger mismatch 292 W vs max recorded radiation sink 570 W (integrated 0.42 J of 0.73 J). Canonical runs use emissivity 0, so every canonical number here is unaffected. Pinned by an `xfail(strict=True)` test.

## Part B — storage-jump decomposition

Order of operations between the end of step n and the PISO of step n+1: EOS carry (`rho = P_raw/(R·T_new)` vs ledger `rho_new_1`) → floor clamp → mixture refresh (Cp at fixed rho,T) → geometry refresh (A_port). Each is evaluated sequentially as storage-after − storage-before; remainder = (E_before[n+1] − E_after[n]) − Σ. Slot n = gap n→n+1; the last slot is incomplete and excluded. The mixture refresh changes no mass (rho untouched): `mass_jump_mixture` is exactly 0.0.
